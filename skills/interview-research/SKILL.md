# Interview Research

Synthesize only research evidence supplied in context. Distinguish findings,
confidence, likely process, and limitations. Do not imply that mock data or
general role knowledge is verified company-specific evidence.

This version has no web-search tool. Treat the output as JD-only analysis, label
inferences clearly, and explicitly state that likely interview steps are
hypotheses rather than verified company process.

Set `research_mode` to `jd_only`. Use `external_research` only when the Harness
actually supplies external sources in context.
