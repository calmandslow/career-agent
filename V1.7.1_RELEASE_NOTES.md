# Career Agent V1.7.1

V1.7.1 turns Interview Answer Coach from a small Mock fixture into a real,
evidence-bounded preparation step.

## Changes

- Passes JD profile, tailored resume, claim audit, question bank, and candidate evidence into Answer Coach.
- Prepares P0 questions first and records question type and evaluation dimension.
- Classifies evidence as sufficient, partial, or insufficient.
- Adds answer skeleton, spoken answer, likely follow-ups, missing information, and answer risks.
- Rejects unknown evidence IDs.
- Forbids a complete answer when evidence is insufficient.
- Adds `qwen_answer_coach.py` and readable `interview_answers.md` output.
- Keeps Qwen3.7 Plus as the core-model route.
